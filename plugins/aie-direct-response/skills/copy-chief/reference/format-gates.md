# Format-specific audit gates

Apply only the relevant gate. A format failure can be Blocking or Major even when the generic score is high.

For a VSL, classify the observed structure before applying a gate. Use reveal timing, number and depth of necessary belief shifts, evidence cadence, and narrative/source burden—not runtime alone. State `short`, `long`, or `editorial long-form`, explain the classification briefly, then apply that gate.

## Sales letter or page

- Lead type fits awareness, sophistication, traffic, and proof.
- Headline/deck, lead, mechanism, product reveal, proof, offer, and CTA form one argument.
- A section-handoff map shows the reader question entering each section, answer supplied, belief earned, and next question opened. Repair unsupported jumps.
- Web pages have a coherent mobile scan path, proof/disclosure placement, repeated but singular CTA, and material terms before action.
- P.S. supports the same decision rather than introducing a new offer or unsupported claim.

## Short VSL

- The hook establishes reader, problem/desire, idea, and reason to continue quickly.
- One proof cluster or demonstration carries the principal promise.
- Product and action arrive without long narrative delay; close density matches the low belief burden.
- Spoken, visual, caption, disclosure, and landing-page claims agree.

## Long VSL

- The script names the necessary belief changes and earns each through explanation and proof.
- Re-hooks introduce a real new question, consequence, demonstration, or evidence.
- The product reveal follows logically from the mechanism; objections and terms are resolved before the close.
- A timed proof-cadence map prevents long unsupported claim runs.

## Editorial long-form VSL

- The discovery or narrative is true, relevant, source-backed, and cannot be removed without weakening the argument.
- The delayed product reveal is earned by necessary belief work, not concealment.
- Escalating evidence increases specificity and resolves skepticism; it does not repeat authority cues.
- “Secret,” enemy, suppression, prediction, or conspiracy framing is supportable and does not manufacture distrust.

## Sales webinar

- Three teaching sections create necessary, connected belief shifts: opportunity/vehicle, internal feasibility, and external feasibility.
- Each shift includes useful teaching, proof, limitations, and a takeaway.
- The pivot transparently signals the offer; the stack uses real deliverables and defensible values.
- Close states fit, price/commitment, process, one action, and any genuine deadline/capacity when one exists.
- High-ticket application includes qualification, disqualification, decision process, and what happens next.
- Q&A has difficult-question handling, prohibited claims, escalation, logging, replay review, and a final concise CTA.
- Recording and participant capture state is explicit: notice, data-use/consent basis where required, chat/Q&A/name/image/voice reuse permission, permitted channels/term, redaction, and actual replay window. Unknown participant privacy or reuse permission is Blocking for replay, clip, transcript, testimonial, or promotional publication.

## Launch email or sequence

- Each email has one decision job, entering belief, exiting belief, proof, and bridge to the next decision.
- Subjects accurately forecast content; sender identity and intimacy are authentic.
- Across the sequence, Big Idea, benefit, claim proof, and product/company/spokesperson credibility are all present.
- Deadline, timezone, offer terms, and CTA destination remain identical across email, page, checkout, and automation.
- Buyers, opt-outs, complaints, and ineligible recipients are suppressed; required consent and technical release evidence is current.
- Essential offer terms and actions are not image-only; a useful plain-text alternative, meaningful links, logical reading order, appropriate contrast, 320px reflow, keyboard-operable destinations, and major-client dark-mode rendering are checked.
- When no genuine deadline exists, late-sequence emails use implementation, comparison, application, proof, objection, or values-based reasons to act; they do not manufacture a close, expiring bonus, or countdown.
