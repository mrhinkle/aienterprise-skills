# Voice, proof, and compliance

## Voice

Create a profile from representative approved samples: role in the reader relationship; tone coordinates; syntax and rhythm; vocabulary; humor and metaphor; signature and forbidden phrases; handling of uncertainty, money, risk, and calls to action. Preserve stable identity while adapting tone to a private letter, recorded narration, or live room.

When samples are absent, use neutral, clear prose and label the voice provisional. Do not imitate a living practitioner. Use named influences only for abstract techniques such as awareness matching, research depth, proof priority, continuity, or conversational delivery.

Run an authenticity red-team against approved samples and records: authority boundaries, worldview, emotional intensity, intimacy, stories, enemies, status cues, and selling posture. Do not manufacture a confession, struggle-to-success persona, insider secret, enemy, or private relationship because the device is common in direct response.

## Atomic evidence ledger

Use the same canonical schema across writer, email, and copy-chief handoffs:

| Claim | Type | Source / evidence owner | Status | Support ceiling | Safe wording | Date / population / denominator | Typicality | Testimonial-use fields | Synthetic-media status / subject permission / disclosure | Disclosure | Approval owner | Release blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Status is `verified`, `qualified`, `unverified`, `contradicted`, or `not applicable` for an opinion, aspiration, or obvious puffery that creates no objective implication in context.

For testimonials and endorsements also record identity/authenticity, original source artifact, exact versus edited or translated wording, date/currentness, permission/release and permitted channels/term, incentive or material connection, typicality support, and approved disclosure. Any unresolved identity, integrity, permission, or disclosure field remains an inline placeholder and blocks release.

For every face, voice, presenter, avatar, demonstration, proof image, or testimonial depiction, record whether it is real capture, AI-generated, or AI-modified; whose identity or likeness is implicated; subject authorization for synthetic commercial use and permitted channels/term; and the required approved disclosure. Never imply that a nonexistent person is a customer, that a real person said or did something they did not, or that a simulation is actual proof. Unknown provenance, permission, or disclosure blocks release.

High-risk claims include quantified outcomes, earnings, health or safety, superiority, “best” or “only,” expert consensus, customer results, time-to-result, scarcity, deadline, savings, comparative price, guarantee, and recurring terms.

Never create testimonials or composite stories that can be mistaken for real customers. Label dramatizations and hypothetical examples so the audience cannot reasonably misread them.

## Publication gates

Detect health, supplement, safety, financial, investment, earnings, legal, employment, housing, credit, children, environmental, and other regulated or consequential categories even when the user does not identify them. Require current category- and jurisdiction-specific review for any detected branch.

- Judge express and implied claims by the net impression of words, visuals, audio, omissions, and sequencing.
- Require appropriate substantiation before dissemination and the advertised level of proof when the copy says or implies it.
- Put necessary qualifications close to the claim and make them perceptible in the same experience.
- Disclose unexpected material connections to endorsers.
- Use only genuine deadlines, capacity, bonuses, price comparisons, and value anchors.
- State trial, renewal, cancellation, refund, eligibility, and material effort or dependency clearly before action.
- Route regulated or high-risk categories to current jurisdiction-specific review. This workflow spots issues; it does not certify legality.
- Include captions for prerecorded synchronized media and plan description or a text alternative for necessary visual information.
- Treat any AI-generated or AI-modified face, voice, presenter, avatar, demonstration, proof image, or testimonial depiction as unresolved until provenance, subject permission where a real person's identity is implicated, and approved context-appropriate disclosure are recorded.

Unresolved material evidence, disclosure, offer terms, or regulated-category review blocks publishable/production-ready status. Label the asset `DRAFT — NOT FOR RELEASE`, name the exact owner/questions, and retain visible inline placeholders.

### Health, supplement, and safety branch

Identify product category, regulator, audience, and jurisdictions. Classify disease, health, structure/function, performance, and safety implications; determine substantiation type and claim ceiling; check labeling/advertising consistency, contraindications and material safety facts, testimonials, and required notifications or disclaimers. A disclaimer cannot cure an unsupported claim. Require qualified review before clean release copy.

### Financial and investment branch

Identify advertiser and speaker status, issuer/adviser/broker context, product or instrument, audience, jurisdiction, recommendation or suitability implications, performance methodology, gross/net and period treatment, hypothetical/backtested status, fair balance, testimonial/promoter conditions, disclosures, records, and approval for live, replay, and edited versions. Require qualified review before clean release copy.

### Earnings, income, and business-opportunity branch

Identify the offer type and whether business-opportunity, franchise, investment, employment, gig-work, or other rules may apply. Require written substantiation, the measured period, denominator across the relevant purchaser population, number and percentage achieving at least the stated result, material participant differences, costs and effort, typicality support, and treatment of atypical testimonials. Do not rely on a generic “results not typical” disclosure to cure a misleading net impression. Require qualified review of any disclosure document, earnings statement, recordkeeping, delivery-timing, and language requirements before clean release copy.

### Environmental branch

Identify every express and implied general-benefit, carbon/emissions, offset, renewable-energy/material, recyclable, recycled-content, compostable, degradable, free-of, non-toxic, certification, or seal claim. Define product/package and lifecycle scope, geography, accounting method, baseline, boundary, time period, and material limitations. Require competent support and clear qualifications; never inflate one narrow attribute into an unqualified overall environmental benefit. Require current jurisdiction review before clean release copy.

## Final red-team

Before delivery ask:

1. What would a skeptical right-fit buyer think this promises?
2. Which sentence would a regulator, competitor, refund requester, or disappointed customer quote?
3. What material fact appears only after the CTA or in a weaker modality?
4. Which proof is merely asserted, cherry-picked, outdated, atypical, or causally overstated?
5. Does the voice remain recognizable when the selling intensifies?
6. If urgency, testimonials, and adjectives were removed, would the argument still stand?
