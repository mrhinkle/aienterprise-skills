# Research basis and practitioner map

The skill synthesizes enduring decision principles rather than imitating any one copywriter or reproducing proprietary templates.

## Classic sources

1. Claude C. Hopkins, *Scientific Advertising* (1923), Library of Congress: specificity, complete salesmanship, product knowledge, traceable response, and test campaigns. https://www.loc.gov/item/23009362/
2. Eugene M. Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising*, Bottom Line Books edition, 2004, ISBN 978-0-88723-298-5: audience awareness and market sophistication as inputs to the lead and claim. Bibliographic record: https://books.google.com/books/about/Breakthrough_Advertising.html?id=vSmitwAACAAJ
3. Gary Halbert, *The Gary Halbert Letter* archive: research, offers, headlines, conversational direct mail, and classic promotions as primary practitioner material. https://www.thegaryhalbertletter.com/
4. Gary Bencivenga, “Marketing Maxim 12”: urgent problem, unique promise, proof, and a user-friendly proposition; the broader archive emphasizes research and proof. https://marketingmaxims.com/maxims/twelve
5. Joseph Sugarman, *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook*, Wiley: preparation, product expertise, sequencing, curiosity, and sentence-to-sentence continuity. https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Adweek+Copywriting+Handbook-p-9780470051245
6. Frank Kern, *Convert 2.0* and official site: campaign as a sales process, useful results before the ask, and contemporary conversational execution. https://www.frankkern.com/
7. Michael Masterson (Mark Ford) and Mike Palmer, *Copy Logic! The New Science of Producing Breakthrough Copy (Without Criticism)*, AWAI, 2009, ISBN 978-0-9821500-1-6: CUB and the Four-Legged Stool. Publisher page: https://www.awai.com/copy-logic/p/
8. Russell Brunson, *Expert Secrets*, Hay House, 2020, ISBN 978-1-4019-6047-6: Big Domino, three belief shifts, pivot, stack, and close. Publisher catalog: https://www.hayhouse.com/expert-secrets-hardcover

Use these as lenses. Do not repeat source copy, claim universal conversion rates, or write “in the style of” living practitioners.

## Synthesis

The durable pattern is research → one message → awareness-fit lead → mechanism → proof near claim → explicit offer → one action → measurement. Long form earns its length only when it performs necessary belief work. Video and webinars require production, accessibility, and modality-aware disclosures in addition to persuasive prose.
