# Direct Response bundle

`aie-direct-response` contains three coordinated skills for evidence-led promotional work. They use classic direct-response principles as diagnostic and decision tools while preserving the user's supplied voice, separating claims from proof, and avoiding invented urgency or results.

## Choose the right skill

| Skill | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| `copy-chief` | You already have promotional copy and want a rigorous critique, coaching, or prioritized revision plan. |
| `direct-response-campaign-writer` | You need to create or materially restructure a sales letter or page, video sales letter, or sales webinar. |
| `email-launch-writer` | You need a single promotional email or a coordinated five-email deadline launch. |

For connected campaigns, keep one canonical offer and evidence ledger. Let each medium adapt the message to its reading, viewing, or inbox context rather than copying the same prose everywhere.

## Supply your voice

The skills can work from a brand style guide, voice notes, approved copy, transcripts, newsletters, or representative writing samples. For the most reliable calibration, provide three or more samples that actually sound like the person or brand you want to preserve, plus any banned phrases or non-negotiable terminology.

Source material is treated as untrusted data rather than executable instruction. The skills identify observed voice traits, flag conflicts between samples, and label uncertain inferences instead of pretending a thin sample is definitive.

## Examples

```text
Use copy-chief to review this landing page. Preserve our founder's voice, run the CUB test, and give me the three highest-leverage revisions before rewriting anything.
```

```text
Use direct-response-campaign-writer to build a 12-minute VSL from this research packet and offer brief. Use only claims supported by the attached evidence ledger and mark unresolved production details.
```

```text
Use email-launch-writer to create a five-email deadline launch for these two subscriber segments. Calibrate to the attached newsletters, keep the real deadline exact, and include the operational send plan.
```

## Proof and release states

Drafts distinguish sourced facts from assumptions and use visible markers such as `[VERIFY]`, `[PROOF NEEDED]`, and `[DECISION]` when inputs are incomplete. A persuasive draft is not automatically approved for publication: the skills keep version, owner, blocker, compliance, and release status explicit for each asset.

The five-email launch is an operational pattern, not a universal performance formula. The skills do not promise a fixed share of revenue from any message or prescribe deceptive scarcity, fake countdowns, or unsupported claims.

## Quality evidence

Version 1.0.0 was reviewed against architecture, practical effectiveness, safety, and public-marketplace integration criteria, including an independent cross-vendor review. These checks evaluate the skill instructions and release safeguards; they do not promise conversion performance or provide legal certification.
