# Five-email “Bank Refill” operating pattern

This is a strong default for a genuine open/close promotion. It is not a universal law and does not justify the claim that a fixed percentage of sales will arrive in the last two emails. Use the sender's own cohort data to validate timing and contribution.

## Sequence map

| Email | Default timing | Decision job | Entering → exiting belief | Core material | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Open / new opportunity | Cart open or first eligible moment | Make the offer and Big Idea understandable | “This may not be relevant or clear” → “I understand the opportunity and whether to explore” | reader relevance, promise, mechanism, offer, fit, CTA | a tease that never says what is sold |
| 2. Proof / story | After enough time to consume email 1 | Increase belief | “I understand it, but doubt it” → “The promise is plausible within stated limits” | demonstration, case evidence, method, limitations, CTA | an invented origin story or atypical result without context |
| 3. Objection / decision help | Mid-window | Resolve the strongest reasonable barrier | “It may work, but not for me or now” → “I understand fit, tradeoffs, and terms” | objection, comparison or FAQ, who it is/is not for, terms, CTA | straw-man objections or a bonus dump |
| 4. Deadline-day decision | Morning in the recipient's relevant timezone | Re-state the complete decision and consequence of delay | “I can decide later” → “I have what I need to decide by the real deadline” | deadline, compact offer recap, strongest proof, logistics, CTA | panic, guilt, or new material terms |
| 5. Final call | Several hours before the actual close | Give a concise last opportunity and exact next step | “I still have time or uncertainty” → “I will act or intentionally pass” | exact deadline/timezone, fit, one key reason, CTA, support path | false “last chance,” extending the offer after claiming closure |

Timing is relative. Adjust for consideration cycle, geography, list expectations, event schedule, and support coverage. First choose and record the actual deadline policy: either one global closing instant, expressed with a named timezone and conversions, or genuinely distinct region-local closes backed by matching checkout behavior. For a global close, every recipient shares the same instant; split send timing only to improve comprehension. For region-local closes, each region's offer must actually close at its stated local time. Never personalize a countdown in a way that implies a different close from actual availability. Confirm every send lands before its applicable close. Do not send two deadline emails merely because a template says so if frequency would violate permission or brand trust.

## Subject-line pattern bank

Generate honest variants from these jobs, not canned phrases:

- direct offer or availability;
- reader problem or desired outcome;
- mechanism or useful discovery;
- evidence or demonstrated result with supportable scope;
- strongest objection or FAQ;
- exact logistical deadline;
- concise personal note consistent with the actual sender relationship.

A subject line must accurately forecast the email. Avoid fake thread prefixes, fabricated account notices, unearned urgency, vague clickbait, and personalization that implies private knowledge or monitoring.

## Adaptations

- **No deadline:** replace emails 4–5 with implementation help, comparison, consultation/application, or a values-based invitation. Do not manufacture scarcity.
- **Waitlist or prelaunch:** separate anticipation from the five-email purchase window and state when the offer becomes available.
- **High ticket:** the CTA may be apply, reply, or book; add qualification and what happens next.
- **Existing customers:** acknowledge ownership and suppress irrelevant beginner education; distinguish upgrade terms.
- **Webinar-driven:** map emails to attended, no-show, replay-engaged, and buyer segments. Do not tell an attendee they “missed it.”
- **Long window:** add useful proof or objection emails only when they have distinct decision jobs. More sends are not inherently better.

## Sequence coherence check

Read only the subjects and CTAs. They should show an honest progression, one offer, and one action. Confirm each entering belief is supported by prior messages and each exiting belief creates the next bridge. Then read each opening and close back-to-back; voice and deadline details must remain consistent. Run a passage-level CUB and specificity sweep. Finally compare price, bonuses, guarantee, eligibility, and expiration against the landing page and checkout.
