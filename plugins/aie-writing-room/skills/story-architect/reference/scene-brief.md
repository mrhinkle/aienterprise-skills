# Scene brief template

Fill one per scene before drafting. If a line can't be filled, the scene isn't ready.

```
Scene:            Ch __ / scene __   (working title)
One job:          <one sentence: what this scene does that no other scene does>
Spine link:       <which part of the spine it advances: cause, cost, thesis, authority>
POV:              <one character, from the config's allowed POV list>
Goal:             <what the POV character wants in this scene>
Conflict:         <what stands in the way; who opposes>
Turn:             <the value that shifts, + to - or - to +>
Who pays:         <the person who bears the cost if this goes wrong>
What changes:     <state of the world or relationship at the end vs. the start>
Enter / exit:     <enter as late as possible; leave before the goodbyes>
End pull:         <the question or pressure that makes the reader turn the page>
Must include:     <clues, facts, callbacks the spine requires>
Must not:         <protected beats not to touch; reveals not to spoil yet>
```

## Example (invented)

```
Scene:            Ch 7 / scene 2   "The second ledger"
One job:          Nell learns the ledger's handwriting is her mother's.
Spine link:       cause (the ledger's origin) and cost (Nell's loyalty)
POV:              Nell
Goal:             Return the ledger to the archive before anyone notices.
Conflict:         The archivist is working late and wants to chat.
Turn:             Safe → exposed: she recognizes a looped "7" she learned as a child.
Who pays:         Nell's mother, if Nell reports what she found.
What changes:     Nell now has a reason to lie to the investigator.
Enter / exit:     Enter at the archive door; exit on the "7".
End pull:         Will she hand it over tomorrow?
Must include:     The water stain on page 40 (a Ch 12 callback).
Must not:         Don't confirm who wrote the entries; handwriting is inference, not fact.
```
