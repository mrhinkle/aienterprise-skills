# Commercial-copy safety and compliance gates

This is a practical issue-spotting checklist, not legal advice. Ask for target jurisdictions and regulated category when they matter. If unknown, flag the uncertainty and avoid jurisdiction-specific assurances.

## US-anchored principles; verify local equivalents

- Evaluate the advertisement's overall or net impression, including implied claims.
- Require a reasonable basis for objective claims before publication. Claims that say or imply a specific level of proof need at least that level.
- Keep qualifications clear, prominent, understandable, and close to the claim. Do not hide a material limit behind a distant link, fleeting frame, rapid speech, or vague label.
- Endorsements must reflect honest experience. Disclose material connections that an audience would not reasonably expect.
- Do not create, purchase, suppress, or selectively present reviews in a misleading way.
- Detect AI-generated or AI-modified faces, voices, presenters, avatars, demonstrations, proof images, and testimonial depictions. Record provenance, any real person's authorization for synthetic commercial use, permitted channels/term, and current jurisdiction-specific disclosure. Do not imply that a nonexistent person is a customer, that a real person endorsed words or conduct they did not, or that simulated proof is real. Unknown or unapproved provenance, subject permission, or disclosure is Blocking.
- Use only real and supportable scarcity, deadlines, quantities, savings, comparisons, list prices, and stack values.
- State material price, renewal, trial, cancellation, eligibility, and guarantee conditions before the reader acts.
- Treat earnings, financial, health, safety, legal, employment, housing, credit, children, and environmental claims as high-risk; require current category and jurisdiction review.
- Do not use shame, threats, disguised ads, obstructive cancellation, or interface tricks to manufacture consent.
- Do not put material price, terms, guarantee, evidence, qualification, or CTA only in an image. Check semantic headings, meaningful link/control names, text alternatives, logical reading order, keyboard access, and comprehension at 320 CSS pixels. For web and email text, target contrast of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text where WCAG 2.2 AA applies.

## Video and live presentation

- Put necessary disclosure in the same modality as the claim when practical; spoken claims often need audible disclosure, and visual claims need readable on-screen disclosure.
- Give disclosures enough duration, contrast, size, and repetition to be perceived in context.
- Mark each scripted claim and testimonial so the producer can carry the evidence and disclosure into slides, captions, lower thirds, and landing pages.
- Plan captions, transcripts, and description of necessary visual information.

## Email

- Do not use deceptive subject lines, sender names, reply cues, or fake thread prefixes.
- Include required sender identification, postal address, and opt-out treatment for the applicable jurisdiction and message type.
- Check consent rules before sending; the U.S., Canada, UK, EU, and other jurisdictions differ materially.
- Ensure a deadline in email matches the landing page, checkout, automation, time zone, and actual offer availability.

Escalate to qualified review when the claim, product category, or jurisdiction creates material legal risk. Never label copy `compliant` solely because it passed this checklist.

## Regulated-category release gate

Unresolved regulated-category or jurisdiction review forces `NOT PUBLISHABLE — SPECIALIST REVIEW REQUIRED`, regardless of score. Name the decision owner, exact claims/questions, evidence needed, jurisdiction, and applicable asset versions.

- **Health, supplement, and safety:** classify disease, health, structure/function, performance, and safety implications; identify product category and regulator; verify substantiation type and claim ceiling, labeling/advertising consistency, contraindication or safety facts, testimonials, and any required notification or disclaimer. A disclaimer does not cure an unsupported claim.
- **Financial and investment:** identify advertiser and speaker status, product/instrument, audience, jurisdiction, recommendation or suitability implications, performance methodology, gross/net and period treatment, hypothetical/backtested status, fair balance, testimonial/promoter conditions, required records, and approval for live, replay, and edited versions.
- **Earnings, income, and business opportunity:** identify the offer type and whether business-opportunity, franchise, investment, employment, gig-work, or other rules may apply. Require written substantiation, the measured period, the denominator across the relevant purchaser population, number and percentage achieving at least the stated result, material participant differences, costs and effort, typicality support, and treatment of atypical testimonials. A generic “results not typical” disclosure does not cure a misleading net impression. Require qualified review of disclosure-document, earnings-statement, recordkeeping, and timing triggers where applicable.
- **Environmental:** identify each express and implied general-benefit, carbon/emissions, offset, renewable-energy/material, recyclable, recycled-content, compostable, degradable, free-of, non-toxic, certification, or seal claim; define product/package and lifecycle scope, geography, accounting method, baseline, boundary, time period, and material limitations. Require competent support and clear qualifications; do not turn a narrow attribute into an unqualified overall environmental benefit. Require current jurisdiction review before release.

## Accessibility release gate

For video, webinar, audio, email, and interactive or image-heavy pages, record `pass`, `fail`, or `unverified` for the applicable access requirements: caption accuracy, transcript equivalence, description of necessary visual information, readable overlays or email contrast, useful plain text, 320px reflow, meaningful links, keyboard-accessible actions/player/controls, pause/stop behavior, motion/flash risk, reading order, and access to the CTA and material terms. Inaccessible or unverified essential claims, terms, or actions are Blocking for a publish-ready verdict.
